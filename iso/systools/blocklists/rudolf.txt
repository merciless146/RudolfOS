! Rudolf OS's own starter list for Oracle's ad and tracker blocking (docs/plan-1.6.1.md Part 10, docs/plan-oracle.md item 5).
! Written for Rudolf OS (GPL-2.0-or-later, as the rest of it): well-known ad-serving and tracking services only, each a whole
! domain whose only job is ads or tracking, so blocking it does not break the sites that use it. In Adblock Plus filter syntax
! (the subset iso/systools/blocklist.py reads: ||domain^, $third-party, @@ exceptions), the same syntax as EasyList and
! EasyPrivacy, which blocklist.py can compile instead (or as well) when they are added to the build.
! Compiled into desktop/orablock61.json:  python3 iso/systools/blocklist.py iso/systools/blocklists/rudolf.txt > desktop/orablock61.json
!
